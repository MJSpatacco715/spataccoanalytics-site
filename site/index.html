How We Work

A messy system that gets used beats a clean system that doesn’t.

We agree. Most “cleanups” fail because they’re someone else’s workflow forced onto your team. Adoption dies in week three.

The cleanest system is the one your team was already building in spreadsheets, sticky notes, and shared inboxes. We just finish the job.
