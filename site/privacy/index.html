Privacy

Privacy Policy. Plainly.

What we collect when you visit SpataccoAnalytics.com, scan one of our QR codes, or use our tools, and what we do with it.

Effective November 27, 2026

Who we are

Spatacco Analytics LLC (“Spatacco Analytics,” “we,” “us”) is a technology and consulting firm based in Bala Cynwyd, Pennsylvania. This policy covers SpataccoAnalytics.com, its tools (including the Website Scorer), and the QR codes on our printed materials. It does not cover work we perform for clients under a separate agreement; that work is governed by the agreement.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details you send us by email, through a form, or by booking a call: name, email address, phone number, firm name, and what you write to us.
  • Website Scorer submissions: the web address you ask us to score and the email address you confirm to receive the full report.

Information collected automatically

  • Site analytics: pages viewed, time on page, referring site, device and browser type, and approximate location (city level). We use this in aggregate to understand which pages and campaigns are useful.
  • QR code and link tracking: our QR codes and some links pass through a short web address on our site (for example, SpataccoAnalytics.com/C). When you use one, we record that it was used, when, the source it was printed for (for example, a business card or a specific event), and basic technical data such as device type. This tells us which materials and events are worth repeating.
  • Server and security logs: our hosting and network providers keep standard logs, including IP addresses, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

LinkedIn Insight Tag

We use the LinkedIn Insight Tag, which places a cookie in your browser. LinkedIn may match visits to its members so we can see aggregate, anonymous information about our visitors (such as industry and job function) and may show our ads on LinkedIn to people who visited our site. LinkedIn does not share your identity with us. You can control this in your LinkedIn ad settings or by blocking cookies in your browser.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, schedule calls, and deliver what you asked for, including Website Scorer reports.
  • To follow up about our services with people who contacted us, connected with us, or requested a report. Every marketing email includes a way to opt out.
  • To measure which pages, materials, and events work, and to improve the site and tools.
  • To keep the site secure and comply with law.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep contact and report information while we have an active or potential business relationship, and for a reasonable period afterward for our records. Aggregate analytics may be kept longer because it does not identify you. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

  • Email: use the opt-out link in any marketing email, or tell us directly.
  • Cookies: block or delete cookies in your browser. The site still works without them.
  • Access, correction, deletion: email us and we will respond within 30 days.
  • Browser privacy signals: we honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals by not loading the LinkedIn Insight Tag for browsers that send them.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), reputable providers, and access controls to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our site and services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the effective date above.
