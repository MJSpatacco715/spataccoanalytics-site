Custom CRM
📇

A CRM built for the way the work is done

Built for my own everyday use and recordkeeping, after growing frustrated with the limitations of industry-leading software. It is shaped to deal work and executive search in wealth management, with access levels from read-only to administrator and a separate portal for buyers and sellers.

  • Reads a resume from a PDF, a Word file or a phone photo, and files each job, date and degree into the person’s record once a user approves it.
  • Fills in firm facts from public SEC filings and license dates from FINRA BrokerCheck.
  • Finds duplicate firms and people and proposes each merge with its reason.
  • Scores buyers against sellers and logs every NDA accepted or declined.
  • Builds an anonymous seller teaser as a PDF from intake answers.
  • Takes in Salesforce exports and sends calls and statuses back.

Multi-user Platform
🏈

A league’s whole season, live and archived

Built for a private twelve-team fantasy football league. Members sign in on a phone, a computer or a TV, and commissioners get controls that members do not. Its archive covers every season from 2005 to 2025.

  • A live scoreboard that keeps itself current through every game window.
  • A live draft room, trades, blind-bid waivers and rule votes, each with its own record.
  • A weekly write-up editor: arrange the blocks, preview the piece, then publish it to the front page and to email.
  • An archive of 1,878 box scores, with records, rivalries and champions drawn from it.
  • A TV app puts live scores over whatever is on, and signs in when a member scans its code with a phone.
  • Installs to a phone like an app, with email reminders for lineups and waiver results.

A season-long golf pick’em

Built for a private group of twelve. Each week every player picks one golfer, and that golfer’s prize money counts toward the player’s season total. No golfer can be used twice.

  • Locks each pick when that golfer tees off, using live tee times.
  • Rebuilds the standings from official results after every tournament.
  • Emails players who have not picked yet and tells the organizer who is missing.
  • A live leaderboard of the group’s golfers during a tournament, with a feed of every pick and change.
  • Installs to a phone home screen like an app.
  • Organizer tools for picks, players and the schedule, with a backup taken before every change.
